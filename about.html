THE BEGINNING

A WINTER
OBSESSION

In 2024, we set out to create faux fur that felt as decadent as the real thing — without compromise. After months of sampling, we found our signature "cloud fur": a dense, feather-soft pile that holds its shape, resists shedding, and wraps you in warmth.

Every cropped jacket, plush boot, and headwarmer is cut and finished in small batches from our London studio. We work with a family-run atelier in the North of England, where each piece is individually checked, brushed, and hand-finished.

The result? Pieces that feel intimate, look bold, and are made to be kept — season after season.
